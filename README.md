# Sasso & Baba - Anniversary Website 💕

A beautiful, romantic website celebrating the anniversary of Sasso and Baba's love story.

## Features

✨ **Home Page**
- Beautiful gradient design with pink, white, and red accents
- Animated background with floating hearts
- Anniversary tagline: "The beginning of a story that continues until the end of life"
- Dynamic counter showing days together
- Smooth navigation to memories page
- Gentle background Arabic music 🎵

✨ **Memories Page**
- Photo gallery with memory cards
- Love message section
- Timeline of special moments
- Responsive design for all devices
- Continuous background music

## Colors
- **Primary Pink**: #FFB6D9

## Music Features
🎵 **Background Music**
- Gentle, soft Arabic music plays automatically
- Music continues across page navigation
- Music control button in bottom-right corner (🔊/🔇)
- Volume set to 30% for comfortable background listening
- Preference saved in browser (continues from last session)

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Music will start automatically
3. Use the 🔊 button to toggle music on/off
4. Navigate through the pages using the buttons
5. Replace placeholder images with your own photos
6. Update the messages and timeline with your special moments

## Customization

### Add Your Photos
Replace the placeholder images in `memories.html` by updating the `.memory-image` divs.

### Update Messages
Edit the love message in the `message-box` section in `memories.html`.

### Change Timeline
Modify the timeline items with your special dates and moments.

### Add Custom Music
Replace the music URL in `index.html` and `memories.html`:
```html
<audio id="backgroundMusic" loop>
    <source src="YOUR_MUSIC_URL.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
</audio>
```
